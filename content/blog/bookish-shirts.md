## What is dark academia?

Dark academia is a style built around old universities and classic literature: candlelit libraries, worn books, autumn colors, ravens and owls. On a t-shirt, it means detailed illustration and a moody palette instead of bright, cartoonish graphics.

> Dark academia is a library at midnight, not a classroom at noon.
