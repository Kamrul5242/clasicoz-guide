A funny Christmas shirt has one job: get a laugh before anyone reaches the snack table. The ones that fail try too hard, with three jokes, tiny text and a reindeer doing something nobody can make out.

**The short answer:** the funniest Christmas shirts use one clear saying that fits the wearer, like a pun on their pet, their job or their favorite way to spend December. Choose the joke they would tell themselves, in lettering big enough to read across the room.

## How to pick a funny Christmas shirt

1. **Match the joke to the person.** A pet, a job or a hobby beats a generic pun.
2. **Keep it readable.** One saying, big lettering.
3. **Think about the room.** Whiskey and Christmas suits an adult party; the Checklist works anywhere.
