## 2. Cat butt coloring book

### What makes it funny

The joke is in the commitment. Someone filled an entire book with cat rear ends drawn in loving detail, then asked an adult to pick the right shade of orange for each one. You get the calm of a coloring page plus the awkward moment of explaining what you are coloring.

Nothing about it is subtle, and that is why it ranks among the best gag gifts for cat lovers. Every cat owner knows the tail-up, butt-in-your-face greeting. This book just turns it into a gallery.

> A cat butt coloring book turns the most familiar view of any cat into a quiet, ridiculous hobby.

### Who it is for

It suits the cat person who laughs at their own pet, the one who tells the story about the butt on the keyboard. It also works for a coworker you know well enough to joke with. Skip it for anyone who likes sentimental gifts, because this one is pure silliness.

- Good fits: friends, siblings, secret Santa swaps
- Poor fits: formal offices, the grandma who frames every cat portrait

### Best occasion and budget

Birthdays, white elephant parties, and stocking stuffers are the sweet spot. Expect to pay roughly $8 to $15, which makes it a low-risk pick among weird gifts for cat lovers. To build a bigger present, add a set of colored pencils and one of our cat mugs for the coffee that goes with the coloring.

## Picking the right gag gift

Every idea on this list works for the same reason. The joke is something your person already says about their cat. When you shop for funny gifts for cat lover friends, match the humor to their personality, not to the cat. A loud sense of humor suits the cat butt bottle opener or the coloring book. Dry and cozy calls for the candle, the pillow, or the tea infuser.

When you are unsure, go wearable. A tee or mug gets used long after a gag gift has had its laugh, so it is the safest pick for a coworker or a relative you cannot read. Check [shipping dates](/faq/) too, because printed-to-order holiday gifts need a few weeks of lead time.
