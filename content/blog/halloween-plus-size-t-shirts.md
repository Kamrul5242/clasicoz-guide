## How to pick the right size

Unisex tees are cut straight, not shaped at the waist, so a few minutes with a tape measure saves a return.

- Lay a tee you already love flat and measure it across the chest, armpit to armpit.
- Compare that number with the size chart on the product page before you order.
- Pick your usual size for a standard fit, or size up one for a relaxed, slouchy look.
- Planning to layer over a long-sleeve top? Size up one.

> Measure a shirt that already fits you, not your body. It is the quickest way to match a size chart.

Every item is printed to order, so check the size twice. The [help center](/faq/) covers returns, which apply to misprints and damaged items.
