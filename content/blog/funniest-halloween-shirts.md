The funniest Halloween shirt is the one people read, get, and laugh at before you finish saying hello. That rules out busy designs with three jokes fighting for space, and it rules out puns so obscure they need a footnote.

**The short answer:** the funniest Halloween shirts use one clear joke, aimed at a specific person: a pun for the word-lover, a checklist for the planner, a job joke for the nurse, firefighter or band director. Pick the joke that matches the wearer, not the one you find funniest yourself.

## How to choose the funniest shirt

1. **Start with the person.** A pun about their job or hobby beats a generic joke every time.
2. **Read it from ten feet away.** If the joke needs a close look, it will not land at a party.
3. **Check the size range.** Every design here comes in S to 5XL, so a whole group can match.
4. **Order early.** Shirts are printed to order and usually ship 3 to 5 days after production starts, so give yourself a cushion before October 31.
